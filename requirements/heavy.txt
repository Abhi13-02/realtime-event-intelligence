# torch is NOT listed here. It is installed separately in the Dockerfile from

# transformers and tokenizers matter most of all: everything downstream is
# derived from their output, and they arrive via sentence-transformers rather
# than being requested directly, so nothing here would have caught a bump.

# production). That bump happened to be harmless — the vectors it produces are
# bit-identical, verified by re-encoding stored articles and comparing at
# float4 — but nothing had guaranteed that, which is the reason for the pin.
#
#   transformers — runs the mpnet forward pass that PRODUCES the embeddings
#   tokenizers   — text -> token ids; different tokenisation, different vector
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
